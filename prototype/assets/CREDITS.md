## Audio

| Asset | File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| "AMBIENCE NIGHT FIELD CRICKET 01.wav" by sengjinn (Freesound user), a one-minute field recording of crickets around midnight | `audio/night-crickets.ogg` | https://freesound.org/people/sengjinn/sounds/175020/ | CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as shown on the sound's page |

The Ogg is derived from the sound's 128 kbit/s MP3 preview, the file Freesound serves without a login. It is converted to
mono 48 kHz, high-passed at 120 Hz, the last 3 seconds are crossfaded into the first 3 so the loop has no seam, the
level is set to -26 LUFS, and it is encoded as Opus at 24 kbit/s. Nothing else is added. The traffic bed and the
whoosh are generated in code and need no credit.
